HOW TO ADD YOUR PROFILE PHOTO
1. Put your chosen image in the img folder.
2. Name it profile.jpg.
3. The home and about pages will display it automatically.
   Alternatively, change src="img/profile.jpg" in those two HTML files.

CONTACT FORM
The form opens the visitor's default email application and prepares an email to
damilareakinlami@gmail.com. It does not send messages through a server. For a
hosted form that submits directly from the website, connect a form service or
backend before publishing.
